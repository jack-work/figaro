# Chat arias: agentless logs, sided grants, studying a log, subscriptions

2026-11-11, chat-aria lane, on figaro main 90c9e0d8 (v0.63.0). Design only, no code.
Source: captains-log 2026/11/11/telegram-aria-herald-two-loops.md, items 1, 2, 4, 5, 6. Gluck's raw text at its bottom wins over the parse. His answers to its questions A to E come through the Engineer; until then this note states a recommendation for each (section 4).

## 1. What exists

**Node kinds and channels.** A node is one figwal trunk of kind `null`, `outfit`, `conversation` or `form`, fixed at mint (`internal/store/xwal_store.go:100-108`; forms-design invariant 1). Channels are `ir`, `form`, `ui` and the per-provider `translations-v2/*` (`xwal_store.go:81-86`, ARCHITECTURE "Streams"). There is no node with a log of its own and no agent.

**How an aria's input and output are stored.** Both live on the one `ir` channel as provider-agnostic `message.Message` records: input as user-role messages, output as assistant messages and tool results. Input enters as `figaro.qua` (`api/rpc/turn.go:21`, `QuaRequest{text, form, images}`), goes into the agent's in-memory inbox (`internal/figaro/inbox.go`), and `TakeReadyUserPrompts` (`inbox.go:284`) folds every queued prompt into the next turn. The queue is spooled to disk only at shutdown (`internal/figaro/spool.go`). Every IR append is fsynced before anyone sees it (ARCHITECTURE "Durability"). Clients read the UI projection, not the IR: `figaro.read` pulls `aria.Page`, `figaro.aria` and `turn.done` push it.

**Forms and SubscribeFrom.** `Form.SubscribeFrom(buffer)` registers first and snapshots second, so nothing falls between them (`internal/store/form_subscribe.go:38`; forms-design §3c). A slow subscriber never blocks the writer: a full buffer counts in `Missed()` and a resync marker rides the next event that fits. Its only caller is the libretto, which copies a studied form (`internal/store/libretto.go:177`).

**The attach lease.** The 08-12 note (captains-log 2026/08/12 form-work-11) planned a lease for each attach. What shipped is narrower: an open subscription keeps its form from idle eviction (`xwal_backend.go:944`) and makes `Reclaimable()` false (`form_tombstone.go:55`). It has no expiry, and the delete does not wait for it ("Deferred reclamation is not built", `xwal_backend.go:1145`).

**The wire.** `figaro.follow {channels: transcript|forms}` (`api/rpc/address.go:18`, `internal/angelus/channels.go`) joins a connection to the node's `ariaHub` (`internal/angelus/hub.go`), which fans out `form.delta`, `figaro.aria` and `turn.done`. Delivery is best effort into a per-connection outbox (`outbox.go`): over 16 MiB, or one write stalled 30 s, and the connection is dropped with "reconnect and resync". Catch-up is client-composed: follow first, then `figaro.form {since}` (`api/rpc/read.go:19`, store `FormSince`) or `figaro.read {sinceLT}`. `fig form listen` does exactly this (`internal/cli/form_listen.go:56`, "Subscribe before seeding"). The web door serves the same JSON-RPC over WebSocket, and `sdk/` is the Go client. So non-figaro clients can already subscribe to forms and transcripts. Two things are missing: a `since` on the follow itself, and a single source of pushes.

**Who pushes form.delta.** A live agent pushes every commit, because its `WatchForm` sink (`internal/figaro/agent.go:265`) sees all writers. A hub-hosted node (dormant figaro, unbound form, role) pushes only from the hub's own `set` path (`internal/angelus/hubs.go:181`). Backend writes that bypass it notify nobody, as read: the role `target-aria` write in cast (`study_hub.go:119`), grants (`box_keys.go:449`), reap marks (`reaped.go:29`), topology (`topology_handlers.go:621`). See H1.

**Study.** `system.studies` on the observer's bound form is the durable truth (`api/form/known_keys.go:65`; `internal/figaro/study.go`). Observation is pull at the stamp: every IR append stamps the observed set's positions into one cursor map (`figwal/xwal/append.go:39`, `AppendMainCursors`, key prefix `libretto:` at `xwal_store.go:1301`). The translator renders each member's fold between stamps as a `system-reminder` (forms-design §8). There is no push and no wake. Study and drop are IR marks that ride the inbox, so they never land between a `tool_use` and its results.

**Incantations.** `system.study_incantation` `{onstudy, onupdate, ondrop}` on the observer's bound form adds a phrase to the event's block (`api/form/incantation.go`). It is decoration (invariant 11). No wake mode exists for studied forms, which answers Gluck's question in the raw text.

**TTL.** `system.ttl` is read from a bound form or an unbound form's state. The deadline is mirrored to the `_meta` sidecar, so the sweep never opens nodes (`internal/store/ttl.go:1-21`). Siblings: `system.keepalive`, `system.retired`, `system.reap_if_empty`. The sweep is `Angelus.expireTTL` (`internal/angelus/lifetime.go:37`) and follows the drawn tree, and `figaro doctor ttl` (`internal/cli/doctor_ttl.go`) explains it. All of these keys are owner-held (`api/form/owner_held.go`).

**Attach and send.** `figaro.attach` proves a node exists without waking it (verb `read`, or `send` suffices: `api/rpc/verbs.go:122`). `figaro.qua` is `send`.

**Authorization.** The verbs are `read listen send fork set create kill admin use join` (`api/rpc/verbs.go`), each method mapped to one. Resources are daemon, aria, sandbox and troupe (`internal/authz/policy.go:20`). Selectors: `aria:` `role:@` `owner:self` `lineage:` `sandbox:` `sandboxed:self` `unboxed` `troupe:` (`policy.go:56`). A grant is `verb:object` (jkauthz). A figaro's effective set is the `[figaros]` ceiling intersected with `system.grants` down its lineage (`internal/authz/figaro_grants.go`; reference/architecture.md "Figaro grants"). The figaro claim (`FIGARO_ARIA`) only narrows, and an unboxed figaro's bash reaches the local door as the owner (#57). Grants cover a whole node. Nothing addresses part of one.

## 2. Plan

### 2.1 The agentless aria: kind `log`

This is a new node kind, `log`, in the runtime layer of the modular-runtime stack. It imports nothing from the agent or the bindings, which is the "aria primitive" that stack already names.

- **Package.** `internal/sidedlog`, its own package from the first commit. It depends on `figwal/xwal` and store's runtime pieces (`Form`, `_meta`) and nothing else. The daemon builds it from the concrete `*XwalBackend` and gives angelus a concrete `*sidedlog.Store` field. It adds no methods to `store.Backend`, no new interface and no type assertion, because S2 deletes `Backend` and the capability interfaces. figwal gets no kind switch (H7). The new kind cases in kill, list and topology live in angelus.

- **Channels.** `in` and `out`, each an append-only list of entries, plus the ordinary `form` channel for metadata and lifetime. An entry is `{seq, at, by, client, client_seq, text, meta}`. `seq` is dense per side and is the watermark. `by` is the writer's principal, stamped by the daemon. `meta` is a small JSON object for the writer's own use (Telegram `chat_id`, `message_id`, `part`/`of`).
- **Why not a conversation with no agent.** The IR is provider input. It carries roles, the `tool_use`/`tool_result` pairing and translator caches, none of which a chat log has. A conversation is also a wake target, and its kind cannot change later. Keeping the log out of the IR keeps herald from forging user-role messages.
- **Idempotence.** Appends dedupe on `(principal, client, client_seq)`, the same rule form writes use (`store.Origin`, `FormLastSeq`). herald passes Telegram's `update_id`, so replaying after a crash cannot double an entry.
- **Lifetime.** `system.ttl` on the log's form. The sweep needs no new rule, only the `_meta` mirror the other kinds carry. A log mints top-level (under the null root), never under the figaro that made it, so `kill -r` of a figaro does not take a chat with it. `--under` places it on purpose. Once the hierarchy guard lands, both placements are edge writes and go through its `Check(principal, plan)` (G4). Nothing here writes parent edges directly.
- **Verbs.** `log.create` (create), `log.append {id, side, entries}` (send), `log.read {id, side, after, limit}` (read), plus the existing `kill`. Fork of a log is refused. CLI: `figaro log new [--ttl 7d]`, `log say <id> [--side out] -- text`, `log read <id> --side in --after N`, `log listen` (2.4).

### 2.2 Sided grants

A grant object takes an optional side prefix: `in:<selector>` or `out:<selector>`. The rest is any selector that exists today. `authz.Resource` gains `Side` ("" is the whole node). To authz, `Side` is an opaque string. authz does not import `sidedlog` and does not know what `in` and `out` mean, and the grammar reserves the prefix and nothing more. A selector with a side matches only that side; one without a side matches every side, so every grant written so far keeps its meaning. Verbs stay the same: append is `send`, entry reads are `read`, following a side is `listen`.

| who | grants on the chat log |
|---|---|
| herald | `send:in:role:@tg-gluck`, `read:out:role:@tg-gluck`, `listen:out:role:@tg-gluck` |
| the serving figaro (`system.grants`) | `read:in:…`, `listen:in:…`, `send:out:…` |

A log addressed through a role keeps working when the chat moves to another log.

**Where the sided check runs for studies.** In the host, never in the bindings. `internal/figaro` gains no authz import, because layer 6 is cutting the four it has (`agent.go`, `form.go`, `server.go`, `study.go`). The angelus study handler checks `read:in` on the log at study time. The wake dispatcher checks it again before it enqueues. The agent receives an already-authorized reader, or an injected `func(observer, log, side) error`.

### 2.3 Studying a log

`figaro study <me> <log> [--side in]` (default `in`). It is recorded in `system.studies` like a form study, so a fork inherits it. The IR mark rides the inbox as today.

- **What the figaro sees.** Not a state diff. At the start of the next turn the agent reads the side from its cursor to the head and appends the new entries to that turn's user message as text, one block per log: a header naming the log, the side and the seq range, then the entries. The `onupdate` incantation adds its phrase. A byte budget per turn applies, and anything over it is announced with the `log read` command that fetches the rest.
- **Watermark.** The cursor rides the same cursor map as a stamp key `log:<id>` → seq. Recovery reads it from the last IR record, with no separate state to lose.
- **Materialized, not derived.** Here this deliberately departs from invariant 8, for logs only. The entries are written into the IR, because a log is reaped by TTL and a translate-time derivation would rewrite past turns (and drop the prompt cache) when it went. Forms keep invariant 8 and librettos. Insertion happens only at a turn's start, so the rule "no out-of-band record between a `tool_use` and its results" holds.
- **Wake mode.** `system.study_wake` on the observer's bound form: `{"<log id>": "wake" | "ride"}`, default `ride` (forms' behaviour). It is a separate key because an incantation is decoration and must never change behaviour (invariant 11). A `wake` study gets a turn when `in` grows. The daemon waits for a quiet window (`system.study_wake_quiet`, default 3 s, restarted by each append, capped at 15 s). It then enqueues one inbox event whose sender is the log, restoring a dormant figaro as `qua` would. The quiet window is also the general fix for Telegram's split pieces: three pieces in two seconds become one turn.
- **Reverse index.** The daemon must find a log's waking observers without scanning. `system.observers` on the log's form (system-managed) is written by study and drop and recomputed by `figaro doctor librettos` (renamed `doctor studies`). A crash leaves it too high, and a stale entry is harmless because the wake checks the observer's own `system.studies` first.

### 2.4 Subscriptions for non-figaro clients

- **One source of pushes.** Each hub with a follower holds one `Form.SubscribeFrom` on its node's form, and that subscription is the only producer of `form.delta`. The agent's `WatchForm` fanout and the hub's `set` notify are both deleted. Every writer now reaches followers (fixes H1).
- **Follow from a watermark.** `figaro.follow` gains `since`, a map from channel to position: `{"forms": 812}`, `{"out": 41}`, `{"transcript": <turn>}`. The server registers, replays `(since, at]`, then streams, so the gap-free order lives on the server and every client gets it free. A buffer overflow keeps today's behaviour (drop the connection, client reconnects with its watermark). The client always holds the watermark. figaro stores no consumer offsets.
- **Logs on the same door.** `log` nodes accept `follow {channels: ["in"|"out"]}` and push `log.entry`. Old daemons refuse an unknown channel name (`followChannels`), so a client checks a capability first.
- **Library.** `sdk/watch`: `Watch(ctx, door, id, channel, from) iter.Seq[Event]`. It reconnects, resumes from the last event it delivered, and hands the caller each position to persist. This is the "library that subscribes and brings it into a runtime". `figaro log listen <id> --side out --from N` prints JSON lines, so herald's outbound loop can be a shell pipeline.
- **"Hub up or not".** There is no offline path. A client that reads the store directly would be a second writer's neighbour on live files. A follow always wakes the hub (not the agent), and `since` covers the "load, then catch up" case.

### 2.5 Hooks stay internal

`WatchForm`, `OnCommit` and `Form.publish` remain in-process seams and are never exported through `api/` or `sdk/`. The public contract is follow, read and `since`. This is enforced as a boundary row in `scripts/check-boundary.sh`: `sdk` imports no `internal/*` at all (true on 664cae6d, so the row costs nothing). ARCHITECTURE says it in one line. Automation (herald, a future bossman library over systemd units) is written against `sdk/watch`.

### 2.6 herald on top (not figaro work)

- **Inbound:** `log.append` to `in` with `client_seq = update_id`. The figaro's wake study does the waking, and herald stops calling `qua`.
- **Outbound:** `sdk/watch` on `out` from its watermark, send to Telegram, persist the watermark after the send. At-least-once: a crash between send and persist resends one message.
- **Paging:** `log read`.

## 3. Steps

Each step ships alone and is green alone. Forms come first because they help today's clients before logs exist.

1. **Hub pushes from the store subscription; follow takes `since` for forms and transcript.** Breaks: a followed dormant form now stays resident while any follower is open (the eviction pin of `xwal_backend.go:944` reaches every hub), so a forgotten `form listen` costs memory until it closes. `form.delta` `At` becomes commit time, not notify time, and hub-path deltas start carrying `Origin`/`Seq`. Hot path: form write fan-out and cold attach need a before/after bench. Nothing on disk.
2. **Kind `log` in `internal/sidedlog`: channels, create/append/read, idempotence, TTL via `_meta`, top-level placement, kill, ls, and its boundary row in the same commit.** The row: `sidedlog` imports no `internal/figaro`, `provider`, `decide`, `authz`, `angelus`, `topo`, and none of store's hierarchy files. It does not wait for S1 to S3, and S3 only moves it. If promote-guard lands first, placement goes through its move plan. Breaks, on disk: a store holding a log node is new to every binary up to 0.63. Rolling back across this step means killing the logs first, and what an old binary does with an unknown kind (listing, GC, topology) is untested (H6). Every switch on kind (`kill.go:27` kills conversations only, `list_handlers.go:123`, `topology_handlers.go:340`) gains a case. The schema registry gains two channels and a generation bump.
3. **Sided grants.** Breaks: the grant grammar reserves `in:` and `out:`. An older daemon refuses a `system.grants` value with a side, and fails closed (it holds nothing). `authz.Resource` grows a field on the Guard path, which runs on every RPC and needs a bench. A `[figaros]` ceiling of `send:*` would let every figaro write every log's `in`, so the default ceiling must change (Q3). The grammar change also sits next to Gluck's open permissions/scopes naming question (a role holds permissions, a binding grants one, `system.grants` is a scope). Both are cheapest to settle together, before the re-sign. El Inspector reads it.
4. **Follow a log side, `log.entry`, `sdk/watch`, `log listen`, the boundary row (`sdk` imports no `internal/*`).** Breaks: nothing on disk. The follow channel namespace grows behind a capability.
5. **Study a log, ride mode.** Breaks: `system.studies` may now name a non-`@` id. A binary up to 0.63 treats it as a libretto and fails, so rolling back means `drop` first. The IR gains text blocks with a log header, which old binaries read as plain text. This is the documented exception to invariant 8.
6. **Wake mode, quiet window, `system.observers`, `doctor studies`.** Breaks: a figaro now turns with no human prompt, which spends provider quota. A flooded chat drives turns, so it needs a cap per window. Each wake counts as access, so a figaro serving a chat never reaps by keepalive. A retired figaro skips the wake and logs it. `doctor librettos` is renamed.

Steps 1 and 4 touch the host. Steps 2 and 3 touch the runtime and authz. Steps 5 and 6 touch the bindings, with their sided checks in the host (2.2). If step 5 turns out to need authz inside `internal/figaro`, that is a hidden variable for El Cantero before any code is written.

## 4. Hidden variables and open questions

- H1. Hub-hosted backend writes (cast's `target-aria`, grants, reap marks, topology) push no `form.delta`, as read. Confirm with a test before step 1, which fixes it.
- H2. The 08-12 "attach lease" is an eviction pin with no expiry, and tombstone reclaim does not wait for readers (`xwal_backend.go:1145`).
- H3. Sided grants bind boxed figaros and claim-stamped calls only. An unboxed figaro's bash is the owner (#57). Real enforcement waits on X2 (sandbox identity).
- H4. The drawn tree drives the TTL sweep and `kill -r` (layers/03). Where a log sits in the tree decides what deletes it. Its placement goes through the hierarchy guard once that lands.
- H5. herald runs on spain and the daemon on gluck. Do the two loops reach the web door with a bearer, or does telefono run them locally?
- H6. Opening a store that holds an unknown kind with an older binary is untested.
- H7. After S4 kind strings are opaque to figwal, so kind `log` must add no figwal switch.
- H8. A single entry is capped by the door frame cap (`frame_cap.go`). Telegram's 4096 characters is far under it.
- H9. Input appended to `in` during a running turn: should it wait for the next turn (as proposed) or ride the next round as steering?
- Q-A. Who writes `out`? Recommend an explicit `figaro log say` (the figaro chooses what the chat sees). A daemon mirror of final prose could later become a study mode.
- Q-B. One log per Telegram chat or per figaro? Recommend per chat, reached through a role that names the serving figaro.
- Q-C. TTL? Recommend 7d, renewable by keepalive.
- Q-D. Should studied input wake? Recommend per study: `wake` for chat logs, `ride` as the default.
- Q-E. bossman: out of scope here. `sdk/watch` is the library it would shrink to.
- Q1. Name and sigil: `log` with no sigil (kind is read from the index), or a sigil like forms' `@`?
- Q2. Does herald's outbound watermark live in herald, or as a key on the log's form (which needs `set` on it)? Recommend herald.
- Q3. The default `[figaros]` ceiling with respect to `in:`.
- Q4. Entries are text only at first. Images later reuse `QuaImage`.
