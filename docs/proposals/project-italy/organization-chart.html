Short-lived work

Fan out the work, not the hierarchy.

An officer can use transient tail forks to parallelize a bounded task. The officer remains accountable, carries context forward and closes the worker after handoff. These workers do not become standing roles.

01 / ASSIGN

Bound the task

Name its owner, deliverable, completion condition and finite TTL before dispatch.

02 / WORK

Use a tail fork

Carry the relevant current context. Coordinate directly with the officer or peer that can unblock the work.

03 / HAND OFF

Refork deliberately

The owner manages context transfer to a fresh fork: decisions, current work, evidence and open obligations.

04 / CLOSE

Retire the old worker

After accepted handoff, preserve the dated record and give the old worker a short TTL, normally ten minutes.
